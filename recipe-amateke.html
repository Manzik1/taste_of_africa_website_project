Amateke

Amateke is a traditional Rwandan dish made from taro roots. The roots are peeled, cleaned and cooked until tender. They are often served as a filling side dish with vegetables, beans or a sauce.

About This Dish

Amateke is enjoyed in Rwanda and is prepared in different ways depending on the household. Taro is valued as a filling root vegetable and can be boiled until soft before serving.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg fresh taro roots
  • 5 cups water (or enough to cover the roots)
  • Salt to taste (optional)
  • Vegetables or sauce for serving (optional)

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash the taro roots to remove dirt.
  2. Peel the roots carefully and cut large pieces into smaller, even-sized portions.
  3. Place the pieces in a cooking pot.
  4. Add enough water to cover them and add salt if desired.
  5. Bring the water to a boil, then cook until the taro is completely tender.
  6. Drain the water and serve the cooked taro warm.

Serving Suggestions

Amateke can be served with beans, vegetables or a sauce. Make sure the taro is fully cooked before eating.

Recipe Information

  • Country: Rwanda
  • Main ingredient: Taro roots
  • Preparation time: 15 minutes
  • Cooking time: 25–35 minutes
  • Servings: 4 people
