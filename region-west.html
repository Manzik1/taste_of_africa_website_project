About West African Food

A region known for rich flavours, colourful dishes and food traditions shared across generations.

West African cuisine is known for its bold spices, rich sauces and filling meals. Countries such as Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire have different food traditions, but many dishes share ingredients such as rice, cassava, yams, beans, peppers, tomatoes and palm oil.

Common cooking methods include boiling, grilling, frying and slow cooking. Meals are often prepared for families and community gatherings, making food an important part of West African culture.

Common Ingredients

  • Rice, cassava and yam
  • Tomatoes, onions and hot peppers
  • Palm oil and groundnut paste
  • Beans, leafy vegetables and okra
  • Fish, chicken, beef and goat meat

Quick Facts About West African Cuisine

  • Rice is a staple food in many West African countries.
  • Palm oil is commonly used in soups and stews.
  • Chilli peppers give many dishes their distinctive heat.
  • Meals are often shared with family and friends.

Other Popular Dishes

  • Fufu and light soup
  • Banku and tilapia
  • Waakye
  • Thieboudienne
  • Attiéké
