Isombe

A traditional Rwandan dish made with cassava leaves, groundnuts and vegetables.

Isombe is a popular dish in Rwanda made from cassava leaves, which are pounded or finely ground before cooking. The leaves are simmered until tender and are often prepared with groundnuts, onions and other vegetables. Groundnuts give the dish a rich flavour and thick texture. Isombe is commonly served with ubugari, rice, beans or other local foods. It is an important part of everyday meals in many Rwandan households.

Cooking Instructions

View Cooking Instructions
  1. Prepare the cassava leaves by pounding or finely grinding them. If using prepared or frozen leaves, follow the package instructions.
  2. Put the cassava leaves in a pot with enough water. Bring to a boil and cook thoroughly until the leaves become soft.
  3. Chop the onions and garlic. Heat vegetable oil in a separate pan and cook the onions until soft.
  4. Add the garlic and diced eggplant. Cook until the eggplant becomes tender.
  5. Add the cooked cassava leaves to the pan and stir everything together.
  6. Mix in the peanut butter. Add a little water if the mixture is too thick.
  7. Add salt and chilli according to your taste. Allow the dish to simmer until the flavours combine and the vegetables are fully cooked.
  8. Serve hot with ubugari, rice, beans or another preferred accompaniment.

Country of Origin

Isombe is a traditional dish from Rwanda, in East Africa. Cassava leaves are widely used in local cooking and are prepared in different ways across the region.

Ingredients

Choose the number of servings to adjust the ingredient quantities.

  • 500 g cassava leaves, pounded or prepared
  • 1 medium eggplant, diced
  • 2 large onions, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3.5 tbsp smooth peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Chilli to taste
  • Water as needed

Quick Info

  • ⏱ Prep: 20 mins
  • 🔥 Cook: About 1 hour
  • 🍽 Original recipe: 4 servings
  • 🌍 Origin: Rwanda
  • 🌿 Diet: Vegan version
