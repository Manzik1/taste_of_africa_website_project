Jollof Rice

A popular West African rice dish cooked in a rich tomato, pepper and onion sauce.

Jollof Rice is a well-known dish across West Africa, especially in Nigeria, Ghana and Senegal. It is prepared by cooking rice in a sauce made from tomatoes, red peppers, onions and spices. The rice absorbs the sauce as it cooks, giving it a rich colour and flavour. Some versions include chicken or other meat, while others are served with fried plantain, vegetables or salad. The ingredients and cooking methods vary between countries, making Jollof Rice an important part of many West African meals and celebrations.

Cooking Instructions

View Cooking Instructions
  1. Wash the rice with clean water until the water becomes less cloudy. Drain and set aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, red bell pepper, scotch bonnet pepper and half of the onion until smooth.
  3. Heat vegetable oil in a large pot. Slice the remaining onion and cook until soft.
  4. Add the tomato paste and stir for a few minutes. Pour in the blended mixture and cook until the sauce thickens and some of the liquid evaporates.
  5. Add thyme, curry powder, bay leaves, salt and black pepper. Stir to combine the seasonings.
  6. Pour in the stock or water and bring the sauce to a boil.
  7. Add the washed rice and stir gently to coat the grains with the sauce.
  8. Cover the pot and cook over low heat until the rice is tender and the liquid has been absorbed. Check occasionally and add a little water if necessary.
  9. Allow the rice to rest for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy with fried plantain, vegetables or grilled chicken.

Country of Origin

Jollof Rice is associated with the Senegambia region of West Africa and has developed into different national versions. Nigeria and Ghana are especially known for their popular preparations of the dish.

Ingredients

Choose the number of servings to adjust the ingredient quantities.

  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 medium tomatoes
  • 1 large red bell pepper
  • 1 medium scotch bonnet pepper
  • 2 medium onions
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 leaves bay leaves
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Quick Info

  • ⏱ Prep: 15 mins
  • 🔥 Cook: 40–50 mins
  • 🍽 Original recipe: 4 servings
  • 🌍 Origin: West Africa
  • 🥗 Diet: Can be vegetarian
